
Code associated with: Responses of springtails (Folsomia candida) to 
copper pollution under challenging temperature and soil moisture conditions

Authors: Ge et al.

Files:
- internal_cu_pb_concentrations.R - analyses of internal metal concentrations
- cu_pb_figures.R - figures related to internal metal concentrations
- survival.R - survival analysessurvival analysis
- growth.R - growth analyses
- growth_figures.R - figures related to growth analyses
- reproduction.R - initial reproduction analyses mainly for exploratory purposes
                    not included in the manuscript
- reproduction_final.R - reproduction analyses
- reproduction_figures.R - figures related to reproduction analyses
- cuaspb_260107.R - initial analyses of internal metal concentrations to 
                    reproduce figures S10 and S14
- sugrwe.R -  initial analyses of life-history traits to reproduce figures S17,
              S20 and S22.

Software:

R version: 4.6.1

Main packages:

tidyverse
emmeans
DHARMa
MASS
effectsize
car

The scripts reproduce the statistical analyses reported in the manuscript.
